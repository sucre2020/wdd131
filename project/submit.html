How submissions are reviewed

Each case is reviewed for clarity, relevance, and supporting evidence. Naijastalled aims to document real public-interest stories without spreading rumor or speculation.

  • We check for public sources or reliable reporting.
  • We verify state, project type, and date details.
  • We add context where details are incomplete.
