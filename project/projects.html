Patterns to watch

Common reasons projects stall

Funding gaps

Projects often stop when capital releases are delayed, diverted, or inconsistent.

Weak project management

Without a strong update cycle, technical and administrative bottlenecks can compound.

Limited public oversight

Opaque decisions make it harder for communities to track costs, milestones, and progress.
